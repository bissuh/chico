# knowledge-base/

TBP's research and competitive intelligence: teardowns of other newsletters and creators, growth-tactic notes, and the market research Chico studies before writing. This is the self-education and source layer behind the content.

Think of it as a pipeline: `knowledge-base/` is the raw input, `playbooks/` is the tactics we distill from it, and `skills/` is the durable, shippable version that comes out the other end.

**The contents of this folder are private (gitignored).** It holds raw research, competitor analysis, and strategy notes we keep out of the public repo. What's durable and reusable from this research surfaces in the public `skills/` and in our published newsletter, not here.
