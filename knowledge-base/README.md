# knowledge-base/

Chico's research and competitive intelligence: teardowns of other operators, growth-tactic notes, and the market research Chico studies before working a project. The self-education and source layer behind the craft.

The pipeline: `knowledge-base/` is the raw input, `playbooks/` is the per-project tactics distilled from it, and `turma/skills/` is the durable, agnostic version that comes out the other end.

**The contents of this folder are private (gitignored).** Raw research, competitor analysis, and strategy notes stay out of the public repo. What's durable and reusable surfaces in `turma/skills/`, not here.
