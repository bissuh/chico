# Faceless Gallery Model — Research Dossier

**Date compiled:** 2026-05-23
**Compiled by:** Chico (4 parallel research threads + source teardown)
**Source that triggered this:** @whotfiszackk X thread, "made $347k last year selling PDFs from pages nobody knows are mine" (`inbox/The Gallery content strategy being.pdf`)
**Purpose:** Document the faceless / no-face gallery-content business model, the money, the distribution, the craft, and the honest odds, so TBP can (a) write an honest edition and (b) launch our own Instagram + TikTok the right way.

---

## 0. The verdict, up front

Three things are true at once, and the guru threads collapse them on purpose:

1. **The model is real.** Selling low-ticket digital products ($10-$127) through no-face content accounts is a legitimate business with ~95% margins. Documented operators exist (Thomas Frank $2.1M, Easlo ~$500k+ in Notion templates).
2. **The odds are brutal.** Median Gumroad product earns ~$364 lifetime. The top 1% of products capture 77% of all revenue. ~42% of faceless creators make under $500/month. The default outcome is near-zero.
3. **The loudest sellers are selling the method, not living off the pages.** Most "$300k faceless" proof traces back to people selling the course/agency about faceless pages. (See the @whotfiszackk teardown in §6.)

**The one lever that separates the 1% from the $0:** distribution + niche specificity + product volume. That is exactly what a faceless IG/TikTok funnel is *for*. The bottleneck in this model is never the product. It is distribution.

**Strategic recommendation for TBP:** do this **openly**, as a documented experiment, branded as TBP. "Faceless" should mean *no-face gallery content*, not *hidden ownership*. A secret anonymous page network would contradict TBP's entire build-in-public thesis (see §6.4). Run it in the open and the brand risk becomes on-brand content. Run it secret and a future reveal kills the thesis.

## 2. Market and money (the honest power law)

**Market size**
- Global digital-goods market ~$124B (2025) → ~$157B (2026 proj), 26.6% CAGR — but this is the big tent (includes gaming). Source: Mordor Intelligence.
- Etsy digital-product slice ~$3.2B (2024) — most relevant comparable, but secondary-sourced (Accio), treat as estimate.
- Gumroad GMV ~$171M (2023), **flat-to-declining since the 2021 COVID peak ($185M)** even as Gumroad's own revenue rose (they raised take rate to flat 10% in 2023). The channel-level pie is not exploding.

**The brutal distribution (this is the publishable truth)**
- Median Gumroad product: **~$364 lifetime**, ~28 sales, $13 median price (InsightRaider scrape of 146k products, ±15-20%).
- **Top 1% of products = 77.3% of all revenue.** Top 10% = 93.8%.
- 34% of products get 0-10 sales. Only 0.5% clear 1,000 sales.
- Gumroad's own 2020 disclosure: of everyone who earned *anything*, ~55% made under $100.
- Multi-product sellers earn **~19x** single-product sellers. Volume is the lever.
- Creator-economy-wide median income ~$3,000/yr; ~47% earned under $500 in 2025; only ~4% clear $100k.

**Platform economics — what the seller keeps on a $20 product**
| Platform | Fee | Keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Gumroad | 10% + $0.50 (handles global tax) | ~$17.50 |
| Lemon Squeezy | 5% + $0.50 (Merchant of Record) | ~$18.50 |
| Payhip Free | 5% + Stripe ~2.9% + $0.30 | ~$18.10 |
| Payhip Pro | $99/mo flat, 0% + Stripe | ~$19.12 minus the $99 nut |
| Etsy | $0.20 + 6.5% + ~3% processing | ~$18.20 (but brings its own traffic) |

Lemon Squeezy / Payhip beat Gumroad on fees at volume. Etsy charges more but supplies demand. Off-Etsy = cheaper but you drive 100% of traffic (that's what the IG/TikTok funnel is for).

**Plausibility of Zack's $345k:** the per-page math is internally consistent (700 sales/yr ≈ 2/day; 2,900/yr ≈ 8/day). But each "winning" page is a top-1% product, and six simultaneously is a portfolio operator who has cracked distribution, not a typical seller. **Real but rare. Survivorship bias presented as a blueprint.**

## 3. Distribution on IG + TikTok (2026 mechanics)

**Instagram**
- Top ranking signals now: watch time, likes-per-reach, and **sends-per-reach** (DM shares = highest-intent action; Mosseri's stated #1 value signal). Saves matter but rank below sends. Shares elevated to top-tier in 2026.
- **Connected vs unconnected reach** are separate. Originality penalties (§7) only throttle *unconnected* reach — the reach a faceless growth account lives on. This is the central risk.
- Format by account size: **under ~50k followers, Reels drive ~2x the reach of carousels** (discovery engine for small accounts). Large accounts (1M+): carousels dominate. Across all sizes, carousels win engagement-per-reach (~6.9% vs ~3.3%) and saves. "Carousel Reels" (swipeable that plays as a Reel) reportedly best of both.
- Practical mix for a new account: ~3-4 Reels/Carousel-Reels (discovery) + 2-3 static carousels (saves/conversion) per week. Carousels up to 20 slides; 6-10 sweet spot.

**TikTok**
- **Photo Mode / slideshows out-reach video** for many niches (each swipe resets the dwell clock). TikTok's own data: carousels ~2.9x comments, ~1.9x likes vs video; some accounts 2-5x impressions. Caveat: shares can drop ~33% on carousels vs video (deep but less viral).
- **Photo posts do NOT earn Creator Rewards payouts** (only original video >1min). Fine for TBP — our model is funnel-to-product/newsletter, not creator fund.
- **US ownership resolved:** deal closed Jan 22, 2026 (Oracle + Silver Lake + MGX ~45%, ByteDance <20%, algorithm licensed + retrained on US data). App is stable but reach norms may drift through 2026 — don't make TikTok the only discovery channel.

**Both platforms are search engines now** (~40% of Gen Z search social over Google):
- IG: keywords in **first sentence** of caption, custom alt text, **3-5 relevant hashtags** (Meta's stated number; the 30-tag spray is dead). Google/Bing now index IG business posts.
- TikTok: keywords in caption + spoken audio + **on-screen text** (all transcribed). On-screen text critical for our voiceless slideshows. Ranking is per-post on engagement, independent of follower count → a brand-new account can rank.

**Funnel:** route bio to **Beehiiv opt-in first** (owned asset), product second. Beehiiv's native Link-in-Bio is free + has conversion analytics → best fit. Clean direct URL (no bit.ly), per-platform UTMs. A benefit-led "subscribe in bio" CTA beats a raw link; comment-keyword → DM funnels cited far higher converting than passive bio (numbers aggregator-sourced, directional only).

**Scheduling (verified):** **Metricool** and **Later** both confirmed to auto-publish TikTok photo carousels + IG carousels via API. **Buffer is video-focused; carousel auto-post unconfirmed — do not assume.** Postiz/Hootsuite/Publer photo-carousel support unverified — test in-app. (Verify all live pricing before spending — Red tier.)

**Growth reality:** expect a **2-4 month slow ramp** before any acceleration; some accounts take ~8 months to 5k. Optimize saves/sends/replies + email opt-ins, not follower vanity. No credible "reach-per-post for a new faceless account" benchmark exists — treat any precise number as a guess.

## 4. The slide / gallery craft

**Carousel/slideshow skeleton (works both platforms):**
```
S1  HOOK     — stops scroll, makes swipe mandatory. Readable in <2s. ~80% of swipe-through.
S2  PROMISE  — restate who it's for + the payoff.
S3  CONTEXT  — the problem / why the old way fails (open loop continues).
S4-7 STEPS   — ONE idea per slide. Whitespace. Never two tips per slide.
S8  PROOF    — number / before-after (partial payoff).
S9  RECAP    — the one thing to remember.
S10 CTA      — Save + Send + funnel ask ("Want this every week? Newsletter in bio").
```
- IG educational carousels best at 8-12 slides; TikTok 5-10. CTA slide lifts engagement ~20-30%.
- **Hook formula:** `[audience signal] + [tension/gap] + [payoff promise]`. #1 failure = informational headline with no tension. Types: Mistake, Contrarian, Numbers, Story (start mid-narrative), How-To (outcome + timeframe).
- **Open-loop / payoff:** each slide partially pays off while teasing the next. The gap is the swipe engine.
- **Faceless brand recognition = design consistency replaces the person.** Signature visual hooks: fixed palette, fixed text-overlay style, fixed logo lockup position, fixed grid. 2 fonts max, 3-5 colors max.

**TBP's Doré engraving canon is ideal for this** (recognizable wrapper, needs no face, teal #2A7A6D + crosshatch reads instantly in-feed):
- Generate the **art wrapper only** in Midjourney using a fixed **`--sref <anchor-image-URL>`** so the whole gallery shares one DNA; tune **`--sw`** (style weight, range 0-1000; `--sw 0` cancels sref; default reportedly ~100 — *unconfirmed, MJ docs returned 403*) once and lock it. V7 retuned sref; old codes may drift (add `--sv 4` or use V6 to reuse).
- **Add all text + the exact teal hex in Canva**, not in MJ (MJ can't render legible text or hit a specific hex). For text-in-image, Ideogram 3.0 is the better engine.

**Batching ("a month in a day") — Canva Bulk Create pipeline:**
1. Generate carousel copy in Claude/ChatGPT as structured rows.
2. Spreadsheet: **1 row = 1 carousel; 1 column = 1 slide field** (`Slide1_Title`, `Slide1_Body`, …).
3. Clean (Find & Replace), export CSV (or Canva Sheets natively).
4. Design ONE on-brand template in Canva → Connect Data → map placeholders to column headers.
5. Generate all carousels at once. Drop in MJ engraving backgrounds.
6. Resize 1080×1920 for TikTok, trim text, add cover frame + audio.

**Long-form → carousels:** atomize **one idea → one carousel**. One newsletter edition → 3-5 carousel concepts, angle-shifted (one data-led, one story-led, one tactical). Cut the full payoff — leave enough curiosity that they want the newsletter.

**Tool stack (costs UNVERIFIED — confirm before spend, Red tier):** Canva Pro (~$10-15/mo, batching + Brand Kit) · Midjourney (engraving wrapper) · optional PostNitro/aiCarousels (AI carousel gen) · Ideogram (text-in-image) · Claude/ChatGPT (copy, already in stack) · Metricool or Later (scheduling).

## 5. The "run both" model

The thread's smartest line: smart operators **run both** — personal brand as the credibility front (partnerships, high-ticket, trust), faceless pages as the volume/revenue backend. For TBP this maps cleanly:
- **Front:** TBP itself (newsletter + YouTube + Bissuh + Chico, build-in-public).
- **Backend:** a no-face gallery IG/TikTok that sells TBP's own products (the skills, lead magnets, templates) at volume.

The data argues *for* this approach: distribution is the bottleneck, not product. A faceless funnel feeding niche-specific $10-30 products, shipped as a portfolio (multi-product sellers earn ~19x), is the documented path out of the median-$364 trap.

## 6. Credibility teardown + the transparency question

### 6.1 @whotfiszackk — skeptical but fair
**Verified:** real active X account ("zack," bio "distributionmaxxing," joined Oct 2025, ~3,809 followers, 435 posts, Telegram link). His verbatim CTA across threads: *"i build info product businesses for people who have knowledge but no infrastructure. if that's you, DM 'ARBITRAGE'."* → **his actual business is a done-for-you agency / lead-gen pitch.** The faceless pages are the lure.

**Red flags on the income claims:**
- Numbers don't reconcile: a Jan 28 2026 thread claims **$140k/month** across 10 accounts; a Feb 21 2026 thread (3 weeks later) claims **$67k/month** across 14 pages, "started 9 months ago." $140k → $67k is a major inconsistency.
- Timeline doesn't fit: account joined Oct 2025 but "started 9 months ago" (≈ May 2025, pre-account).
- Flagship threads are explicitly **hypothetical** ("operator could be running this from Bali…", invented math).
- Everyday posts get 10-34 likes / ~1-2.7k views despite a claimed $67k+/mo operation; timeline is heavy reposts of other growth accounts.
- **Zero independent verification** in either direction. No receipts.
- **The exact "$347k" thread could not be located/verified.** Themes match him; the specific $347k figure is unconfirmed.

**Fair read:** a real person running a real *agency/services* business whose marketing is highly produced, internally inconsistent, partly hypothetical faceless numbers. Far more credibly "selling the dream + an agency" than "documented operator with an audited $67-140k/mo empire." **Treat his revenue figures as marketing, not data.**

### 6.2 What's actually documented
- Strongest real cases (Thomas Frank $2.1M, Easlo ~$500k+) **had an audience first.** The "faceless from zero, no audience" success story is far rarer than threads imply.
- The "famous front + secret faceless empire" pattern is mostly a rhetorical device — found portfolio-of-faceless-pages operators (e.g. Dominic / @creatorstrategies, a Stan marketing case study), not a verified celebrity-with-hidden-empire.

### 6.3 2026 trend: saturating AND being squeezed
- IG **originality crackdown effective Apr 30, 2026**: accounts posting unoriginal reels/photos/carousels get cut from non-follower recommendations. Watermarks/credits/borders/screenshots don't count as original.
- **TikTok actively bans faceless commercial accounts** using identical AI voices + recycled stock + hyped captions.
- **YouTube demonetizing "slop"** (auto-voiceover, templated slideshow, faceless AI clips); CEO named it a 2026 priority.
- **Anti-AI / "100% human" backlash** is a predicted 2026 theme.
- Direction: AI-as-accelerator-inside-real-work wins; AI-as-full-replacement gets demonetized. Pure-automation faceless is squeezed precisely as it gets popular.

### 6.4 The transparency question (decisive for TBP)
TBP's thesis is transparency + building in public + "a regular person can do this, watch us." A **hidden** faceless network is the photographic negative of that promise — if it ever surfaced that TBP preaches transparency while secretly running anonymous AI product pages, the thesis dies. And in 2026's anti-slop climate, "secret AI faceless pages selling PDFs" is close to the worst possible reveal for a trust brand.

**The honest framing that keeps the upside:** run it as an **open** experiment. "We're testing whether the faceless-gallery model actually works. Here's the page, the real numbers, what broke." That converts brand risk into on-brand content and lets us publish the truth either way. *Faceless = no-face content format, branded openly as TBP. Not anonymous ownership.*

## 7. Risks (consolidated)
1. **Survivorship bias** — the default outcome is ~$0. Lead any reader-facing content with this.
2. **IG originality throttling** (Apr 30 2026) — original creative only; strip watermarks before cross-posting; never screen-record TikTok → IG. Because TBP makes its own content, the penalty doesn't apply *if* we keep it original.
3. **Shadowban** is now a formal status (Settings → Account → Account Status). Triggers: spammy short-link bios, mass DMs, banned hashtags, ToS-violating automation.
4. **External-link reach drag** — keep CTA "link in bio," don't stuff URLs in captions.
5. **Platform dependency** — Meta's 2025 mass-ban wave, TikTok ownership/algorithm churn. Convert to the owned Beehiiv list as fast as possible. Rented discovery → owned audience.
6. **AI-slop saturation + buyer pickiness** — generic AI templates are invisible. Edge = niche specificity + real usefulness + distribution.

## 8. What's real vs hype (publishable)
**REAL:** model works, ~95% margins, seller keeps ~87-92%, documented six/seven-figure cases, a focused multi-product operator *can* hit $300k+.
**HYPE:** "passive income / anyone can do it" (median $364 lifetime); big cases had audiences first; most "$300k faceless" proof is people selling the course; AI commoditized the easy stuff rather than opening a goldrush; Gumroad GMV flat since 2021.
**Honest TBP line:** real model, brutal power-law payout, one lever (distribution + niche + volume). Sell the *method and the odds*, not the dream.
