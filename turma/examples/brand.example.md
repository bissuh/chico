## 1. The project
- **Name:** Ember & Ash
- **One-liner:** Small-batch hot sauce built around single-origin chiles, sold direct.
- **Stage:** launched, ~400 orders/month

## 2. The audience
- **Who:** home cooks 28-45 who follow food creators and treat condiments as a hobby, not a commodity
- **The splinter:** grocery hot sauce is either boring vinegar or novelty-hot with no flavor
- **Where they are:** Instagram Reels, TikTok food-tok, r/hotsauce, YouTube cooking channels
- **What they already tried:** the big "extreme heat" brands, and got burned with no taste

## 3. The goal
- **Primary conversion:** first purchase (the 3-bottle starter box)
- **Secondary:** email opt-in for the ones not ready to buy
- **What winning this quarter looks like:** 400 -> 700 orders/month, 25% from repeat buyers

## 4. The offer
- **Free / lead magnet:** "The Heat-to-Flavor Map" (one page, matching chile to dish)
- **Paid:** 3-bottle starter box $29, monthly club $19/mo

## 5. Voice
- **Register:** warm, confident, a little cheeky. Food-nerd who won't gatekeep.
- **Adjectives:** flavor-first, honest, playful, specific
- **POV:** brand "we," founder shows up on camera
- **Sample right:** "Heat is easy. Flavor is the hard part, and it's the only part that matters."
- **Sample wrong:** "Unleash the ultimate flavor experience and elevate your palate."

## 6. House style / banned
- **Dashes:** off in public copy
- **AI tells:** the usual (furthermore, leverage, delve, elevate)
- **Never say:** "world's hottest," fake scarcity, health claims about capsaicin
- **Compliance:** no medical claims, allergen info accurate on every product post

- [ ] SEO / blog (later)
- **Primary 1-2 for now:** IG/TikTok video + email
